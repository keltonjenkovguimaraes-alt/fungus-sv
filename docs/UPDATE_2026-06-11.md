# FUNGUS-SV Update — June 11, 2026

## Multi-Strain Validation Complete (6 Strains)

Completed ICB consensus + triangulation validation for CICC-1445 against all 6 reference strains.

### Results Table

| Strain | Species | Type | Total SVs | DEL | DUP | INV | Mean T (>5kb) |
|--------|---------|------|-----------|-----|-----|-----|---------------|
| S288C | *S. cerevisiae* | Laboratory | 285 | 254 | 19 | 12 | 0.773 |
| BJ4 | *S. cerevisiae* | Industrial | 176 | 151 | 14 | 11 | 0.441 |
| SX2 | *S. cerevisiae* | Industrial | 310 | 283 | 18 | 9 | 0.536 |
| IMX2600 | *S. cerevisiae* | Engineered | 345 | 313 | 20 | 12 | 0.800 |
| Makgeolli | *S. cerevisiae* | Fermentation | 244 | 218 | 17 | 9 | 0.719 |
| **Jurei** | ***S. jurei*** | **Wild Type** | **167** | **156** | **11** | **0** | **0.865** |

**Total: 1,527 SVs across 6 strains.**

### Key Findings
- CICC-1445 closest to BJ4 (176 SVs) — both Chinese industrial strains
- Most divergent from IMX2600 (345 SVs) — engineered lab strain
- **Jurei (wild type, different species) has the fewest SVs (167) but highest mean T-score (0.865)** — fewer but cleaner structural variants
- Jurei shows zero inversions — consistent with cross-species comparison
- DUP_SPLIT_READ_CONFIRMED and INV_SPLIT_READ_CONFIRMED overrides working across all strains
